# Quick guide

**Sign in** with your office username (the range name in lower case, e.g. `himatnagar`, or `division`) and the temporary password from the credentials file given to you. Change it under *Password* after the first sign-in.
**Financial year**: the box in the top bar. All screens show the selected year (2026-27 is the current one).

## Range office
- **All Vouchers** - everything entered for your range. Search by party, bill no., description, advice/voucher no.; filter by type, month, budget code, treasury status. Open a voucher to see items, amounts and earlier versions; **Print voucher** gives a printable page.
- **New voucher**: the green "+ New ..." buttons on the Vouchers page (or the *Voucher Entry* menu). Pick the budget code (scheme fills in), round -> beat -> place, pick the party/beneficiary/D.Wager from your register, add work items; totals are calculated as you type. **Copy entry** repeats an earlier voucher.
- **Edit / Delete** a voucher from its page - possible until the Division puts it in an advice. The old version is always kept.
- **Allotment vs Expenditure**: your allotment, data entered and what is left, per budget code.
- **Registers & Rates**: your parties, beneficiaries, D.Wagers; SOR, tender rates, estimates.

## Division office
- **All Vouchers** for all ranges (filter by office).
- **Process bill**: *Not yet received* tab - mark vouchers received; *Ready to process* tab - select vouchers, choose the bill type and date, create the advice. Open an advice to enter treasury details, GST challan no., change date / bill register / order no., assign final voucher numbers, or delete it (not once the treasury approved it).
- **Allotment from Circle / to Range / Adjustment**, **L.C. entries**, **Cash account**, **Party registration (tender parties)** as before.
- **Allotment vs Expenditure** and **Registers & Rates -> Budget position** show circle, adjusted, distributed and pending allotment per budget code.

## Good to know
- Allotment from Circle: the **newest** entry of a budget code is its current allotment (entries are cumulative restatements, they are not added up).
- Vouchers marked **"older export only"** exist in an earlier export but not in the latest register; please review them and delete any that should not exist.
- The report screens marked *Not yet connected* will be switched over to the migrated data next.

## Two financial years (added 25 Nov 2026)
The header switcher shows **2025-26** and **2026-27**; every screen follows it. 2025-26 holds 17,704 vouchers, its allotments, cash account, cheque register, SF beneficiaries and the D.Wager salary / arrears registers (Registers & Rates -> *D.Wager salary register*, *D.Wager arrears register*, *Deduction cheques*). Pay slips of 2025-26 show days worked, leaves and basic pay from the D.Wager salary register. 2025-26 records the treasury result by month only (no date) and uses payment modes IFMS and PFMS.
