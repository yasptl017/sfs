## Rules proven against your spreadsheets (do not change without re-checking)
- **Circle allotment** of a budget code = its **latest** entry (by its own time stamp). Entries are cumulative restatements; adding them is wrong. 282/282 codes.
- **Allotment to a range** = **sum** of its entries (increments, negatives allowed). 3,948/3,948 code x range cells.
- **Adjusted allotment** = latest circle + adjustments in - adjustments out. **Pending** = adjusted - distributed to ranges.
- **Expenditure booked** = payable of vouchers that are in an advice. **Data entered** = payable of vouchers that are not rejected. 3,948/3,948 cells each (the 7 "only in older export" vouchers are the only difference).
- **Voucher amounts**: item amount = typed amount (quantity x rate only when blank - survival-based payments rely on this); sub total = sum; approved = sub total x approved %; payable = approved + SGST + CGST + IGST + labour cess + adjustment; net = payable - deductions. Reproduces the stored amounts of the 7,572 vouchers (approved 100%, deductions 100%, net 100%, payable 99.99% - the one exception is the known Modasa Sr 51 inconsistency).
- **Advices**: IFMS advices numbered 1.., SNA advices 5001..; bill register no = "advice no/financial year"; final voucher nos. SR- / CONT- / SNA- series that **restarts every month** (month of the treasury approval; verified: each month runs 1..n). A voucher must be "Received at Division" before it can be advised.

## Voucher Print (range of vouchers, Form No. 35) - ported (step 1 of the remaining six)
Reads the typed vouchers of the signed-in range and financial year. Real data only: serial numbers that do not exist are listed as "not found" (the legacy code invented sample vouchers with placeholder PAN/place). Voucher no. = the final voucher number (SR-/CONT-/SNA-) once assigned, else "Sr. n". Deductions come from the stored deduction columns (SGST/CGST/IGST/labour cess/deposit/TDS; GPF, CPF, P.Tax, group insurance and other are grouped as "other"). Length/breadth/depth are not recorded on vouchers and print as "-". The Division office's own vouchers cannot be printed here (range-only screen, as before): use *All Vouchers -> Print voucher*.
Verified on all 7,278 range vouchers (`verify_reports.php voucherprint`, 11 checks) + `VoucherPrintTest` (5 original, fixtures changed to vouchers, + 4 new).

## Abstract Print (monthly voucher abstract of a range) - ported (step 2 of the remaining six)
Reads the typed vouchers of the signed-in range and financial year, filtered by entry month and docket no. Dockets in the picker and the "recent abstracts" table are the real month x docket batches (the legacy code showed invented DOC/2026/... rows and, for an empty month, four invented sample parties). A month/docket with no vouchers now prints "nothing to print". Gross = payable, deductions = stored total, net = stored net; the budget-head table groups by budget code. Quantity/rate print "-" when not recorded; length/breadth/depth and work dates are not recorded on vouchers. The Division's own vouchers are not on this range-only screen (as before).
Verified (`verify_reports.php abstract`, 13 checks): 82 range x month abstracts and 1,590+ range x month x docket abstracts equal the database in count and net; gross/net/breakdown totals; all 7,278 vouchers balance; months add up to each range's total; no placeholder text. `AbstractPrintTest`: 5 original (fixtures changed to vouchers) + 2 new = 8 pass.

## Work Order print (range) - ported (step 3 of the remaining six)
A work order is printed for each voucher of the range/month/docket that has a **recorded work order number** on one of its items (4,742 of the year's range vouchers; mostly Tender, a few WL Bene.). Order no. = the item work order no(s); start/end dates = the recorded item work start/end dates (completion period shown = recorded end + the "extend by N days" you choose; the legacy "start + 15 days" guess was removed - if no end date is recorded the field stays blank). Outward no. = the voucher's Bill Order Outward No. Quantity/rate print "-" when not recorded. Vouchers without a work order (Free, salary, arrears, most Bene.) are not printed; the screen says how many were skipped ("N voucher(s) ... have no work order recorded"). The invented sample parties, demo dockets and fake WO/outward numbers are gone.
Verified (`verify_reports.php workorder`, 11 checks): 82 range x month and 1,197 range x month x docket selections equal the database in count and amount; dates of all 4,742 work orders equal the recorded dates; nothing silently dropped; no placeholder text. `WorkOrderPrintTest` 5 original (fixtures -> vouchers) + 2 new = 7 pass.

## Vavetar register (range) - ported (step 4 of the remaining six)
Plantation expenditure register per round / beat / place, from the range's non-rejected vouchers of the selected year (voucher place/round/beat, compared trimmed; a blank round or beat means "all"). **Date wise**: every voucher item by work start date (else work-order date, else bill date) with a progressive total. **Work wise**: the same items grouped by work description (the legacy keyword-guessed categories were removed). Total = sum of item amounts (before GST/deductions). Area and year of creation come from the range's location list when one place is chosen (else "-"). The picker offers the location list plus every place the vouchers really use (28 used places were missing from the list), and the quick-pick table shows the 12 places with most expenditure. Invented demo plantation rows and the fake "North Round / Compartment 12" fallback are gone. 
Verified (`verify_reports.php vavetar`, 12 checks): all 761 range x place selections equal the database in voucher count, expenditure, item count, progressive end total and work-wise totals; every used place is offered; no placeholder text. `VavetarRegisterTest` 5 original (fixtures -> vouchers) + 3 new = 9 pass.
Note: 15 vouchers of one range have no beat recorded; they appear when the beat is left blank.

## SOR limit report (range) - ported (step 5 of the remaining six)
Every non-rejected voucher item of the range/year that carries a rate-list code (8,313 items in the 15 range logins) is compared with the sanctioned reference found in the data:
- **Rate**: the dated SOR rate in force on the item's work date (latest effective date on or before it), else the approved tender rate, else the estimate rate. An item billed above it is "Exceeded Limit".
- **Quantity / amount limit**: only where an estimate line exists for the code (e.g. the EN03 codes): the estimate quantity and amount, compared with the range's **cumulative** billing of that code over the year (not only the filtered month). Over the estimate amount = "Exceeded Limit"; 90%+ = "Critical".
- **No reference**: codes that are in no rate list (841 items, e.g. the month-named salary codes) are listed as "Not in rate list" with "-" - they are not counted in the compliance rate.
The legacy report's invented "standard SOR master" (SOR-01..), its 15% "sanctioned quantity" margin and its sample rows were removed; so was the default "Aug / first budget code" filter (blank now means all). Items with no rate-list code at all (free entries, most salary and beneficiary items) are not part of this report. Fin. year follows the header switcher.
Result on the real data: 22 items exceed their reference (rate or estimate limit).
Verified (`verify_reports.php sorlimit`, 13 checks): item counts and billed amounts per range, an independent recomputation of the reference rate and the violation flags of all 8,313 items, estimate limits/cumulative billing, violations mode, CSV rows, no placeholders. `SorLimitReportTest` 6 original (fixtures -> vouchers) + 4 new = 10 pass.

## Final voucher nos. / Form No. 35 / Cash book (Division) - ported (step 6 of 6: ALL legacy reports are now on the migrated data)
- **Final voucher numbers** restart every month: each series (SR-, CONT-, SNA-) of a treasury-approval month runs 1..n (checked on the whole year). The old implementation here (and the "Final voucher nos." button on an advice) numbered one running series for the whole year; both now use `App\Support\FinalVoucherNumbering` (continues after the highest number of the same series and month; every number leaves an audit version). **Assign Final Voucher Nos** (month + mode) numbers the approved vouchers that still have none: today 111 approved vouchers (SNA advices 5006-5017..., Jul/Aug/Oct) are unnumbered - nothing was changed on your data; the button does it when you click it. (In the sheets some SNA numbers are shared by two vouchers; new numbers are one per voucher.)
- **Form No. 35 / sorted vouchers**: the treasury-approved vouchers of the month and mode (IFMS/SNA), range or all ranges, in final-number order (SR, CONT, SNA; unnumbered last, marked "(not numbered)"). Gross = payable, deductions = stored total, net = stored net; date = bill date. Scheme names can be merged into groups (screen "Merge schemes", now saved in `division_scheme_groups`; the invented default groups are gone) and the group name prints instead of the scheme.
- **Cash book** (all modes - L.C. is not split by mode): receipts = L.C. issued in the month, payments = payable amount of vouchers approved in the month, opening = L.C. - payments of the earlier months of the year, closing = opening + receipts - payments. The **opening balance of the year is not recorded** in your sheets, so the book starts from nil (the legacy report showed an invented 1,50,000 opening); this is stated on the print. A range cash book shows payments only (L.C. is issued to the Division). Year end: L.C. 393,023,464 - payments 310,260,448 = 82,763,016 (undisbursed).
- Title page / sticker / certificate / credit / debit parts keep their layouts. The invented sample parties, fake FV-001.. numbers, dummy GSTIN and demo "recent assignments" are gone; the index lists the real month x mode batches (approved vs numbered).
Verified (`verify_reports.php cashbook`, 16 checks): 24 month x mode schedules and 12 cash-book months equal the database; year total = Passed Vouchers header (310,260,448 / 7,521,794 / 302,738,654); numbering order; balance roll-forward; each of the 15 ranges; assignment tested in a rolled-back transaction (60 SNA vouchers of Jul -> SNA-93..152). `FinalVoucherCashbookTest` 6 original + 5 new = 11 pass.
